about Armonia

An ideal destination for your vacation throughout the year with the best options for a family. It has a tidy fenced yard so children can play safely. It is also an essential starting point for bicyclist, mountain hiking, mountain biking, for lover cyclists, excursions or those who want to enjoy and explore nature on walks.

With respect to the environment Villa Armonia Boutique Living has implemented the following:

– The disinfection of pool water is done by salt electrolysis ecological cleaning without chemicals

– TV (LED) Low consumption

– Solar collector and heat pump water heating support

– The A/C facilities are latest technology

– Jacuzzi with Active Oxygen

– We use degradable toilet paper

– There is also a recycle bin for newspapers, plastic, aluminum

– Linen is changed every 7 days (Without extre charge)

– We use friendly to the environment and human cleaning materials

– We use low energy bulbs

– Planted garden plants adapted to the natural environment

– On the outside we have placed light sensors and timers for the evening lighting
